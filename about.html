About

Codex Echo Systems LLC is a Texas technology company focused on the development of practical software applications and digital systems. We emphasize clear functionality, disciplined development, responsible use of technology, and products designed around real-world needs.

Founder

Terry T. McGovern is Founder of Terry McGovern Holdings LLC and its operating companies. A United States Marine Corps veteran (1996–2005) who served as an aircraft maintenance mechanic, shop lead, and quality assurance representative, he holds a Bachelor of Science in Professional Aeronautics and a Master of Science in Management from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University, with a Certificate in Safety and double minors in Aviation Safety and Management. His civilian career includes aerospace logistics engineering at Bell Helicopter and principal systems-engineering product leadership at SAIC. He is the author of Think, Feel, Believe: How Your Brain Writes Your Story.
